The Wire collects headlines every hour from editor-approved Sudanese, regional, international and humanitarian outlets. We link to the original reporting and never republish article text. Reports of the same event from several outlets are grouped into one story; top stories are ranked by how many outlets covered them in the last 48 hours.

Headlines from Arabic-language outlets are machine-translated into English (marked AR→EN) and are not reviewed by an editor before they appear. Translations can contain errors; follow the link to read the original. Places are tagged automatically from the original headline and may occasionally be wrong.
